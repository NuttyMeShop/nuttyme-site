Business Activity

NuttyMe LLC develops and operates subscription-based consumer software applications. Products are distributed on major mobile platforms (iOS, Android) and the web, with a focus on artificial intelligence-driven user experiences.

The company operates on a freemium subscription model, offering core functionality at no cost and premium features through monthly and annual subscriptions.

Products

ORA

ORA is an AI-powered personal clarity application that helps users reflect on decisions and situations through guided AI-based reasoning and structured symbolic frameworks. The application is available on iOS and Android and supports multiple languages. ORA is currently in final testing prior to public release.

Nanabu

Nanabu is a music-based English language learning application that teaches English through structured song-based lessons, progressing from beginner to advanced levels. Nanabu is currently under development, with planned launch in 2026.

Revenue Model

Both applications operate on a freemium subscription model:

  • Core features — free access
  • Premium features — monthly and annual subscriptions

The company is currently in a pre-revenue launch phase.

Management

Founder & Manager Jean-Michel Grau

Jean-Michel Grau is the founder and managing member of NuttyMe LLC. He has over 25 years of experience in software development, operations, and business building across multiple countries. His background includes leadership roles in language education businesses in Japan, where he developed internal software systems, expanded operations, and led revenue growth initiatives.

In 2023, he founded NuttyMe LLC to build consumer software products, including ORA and Nanabu. He is fluent in Japanese, English, and French.
