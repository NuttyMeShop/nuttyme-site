Business Activity

NuttyMe LLC develops and operates consumer-facing digital applications distributed through major mobile platforms (iOS, Android) and the web.

The company's current portfolio includes two products:

ORA — A personal clarity and decision-support application. ORA is currently completing final testing and is scheduled for commercial release on the Apple App Store and Google Play. The application will be available in seven languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Japanese.

Nanabu — A music-based English language learning platform. Nanabu is in active development with a planned commercial launch in 2026.

Revenue Model — Both applications will operate on a freemium model: core features available at no cost, with premium functionality offered via monthly and annual subscription.

Current Status — The company is in its pre-revenue launch phase. The founder is actively managing operations, product development, and go-to-market execution.
