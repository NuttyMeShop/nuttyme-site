Business Activity

NuttyMe LLC is a U.S.-based technology company that develops consumer-facing SaaS applications distributed on iOS, Android, and the web. The company operates at the intersection of artificial intelligence, wellness technology, and educational software.

ORA is an AI-powered personal clarity and decision-support application using symbolic and reflective frameworks. Available in seven languages on iOS and Android, it helps users gain perspective on decisions through guided reflection. ORA is completing final testing ahead of its commercial release.

Nanabu is a music-based English language learning platform. It teaches English through 180 songs structured as progressive lessons, from beginner to fluent expression. Nanabu is in active development with a planned launch in 2026.

Revenue Model — Both applications operate on a freemium subscription model. Core features are available at no cost; premium functionality is offered via monthly and annual subscriptions. The company is in its pre-revenue launch phase.

Management

Founder & Manager Jean-Michel Grau
Mailing Address 400 Rella Blvd, #207-298, Montebello, NY 10901, USA

Jean-Michel Grau is the founder and managing member of NuttyMe LLC. With over 25 years of experience in software, operations, and business building across three continents, he is a hands-on builder and operator.

He began his career in logistics at Disneyland Paris, then transitioned into web development during the early internet era — building his first websites as a self-taught developer. He spent over a decade in Japan's language education sector, where he led business development, opened three new branches, built a custom management application, and increased revenue by 50% as the school's director. He has founded multiple ventures: a digital strategy firm (CaritasNet), a cross-cultural consulting firm (Advantage Solutions), and co-founded a French takeaway chain in Tokyo that scaled to four locations.

In 2023, he founded NuttyMe LLC to develop his own products — starting with ORA and Nanabu. He holds a Certificate of Applied Japanese Language from the Yoshida Institute and a Warehouse Management degree from AFT-IFTIM, France. He speaks Japanese, English, and French.
