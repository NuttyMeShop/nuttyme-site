How ORA works

ORA combines large language models with structured pedagogical frameworks to deliver personalized tutoring at scale. Whether a learner is mastering a new language, preparing for standardized tests, or exploring a technical subject, ORA meets them at their level and accelerates progress.

Diagnostic Assessment

ORA begins by evaluating the learner’s current knowledge to establish a personalized baseline and identify knowledge gaps.

Adaptive Pathways

Lessons, exercises, and reviews are sequenced dynamically based on real-time performance and learning velocity.

Conversational Support

Learners ask questions in natural language and receive contextual explanations, examples, and feedback instantly.

Use Cases

  • Language Learning — Vocabulary, grammar, and conversation practice with instant correction.
  • Test Preparation — Structured review for academic and professional examinations.
  • Professional Development — Upskilling in technical and business domains with curated content paths.
  • K-12 & Higher Ed — Supplemental tutoring aligned with standard curricula.
