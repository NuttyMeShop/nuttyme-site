About ORA

ORA is a mobile and web application that helps users make clearer decisions through guided reflection and symbolic prompts. The app combines 78 tarot cards with an AI trained on symbolism to deliver personalized, contextual readings. Each reading adapts to your question, your context, and your moment.

ORA is not a fortune-telling app. It is a decision companion. The cards do not tell what will happen — they reveal what is already there, and what you may not yet see.

The ORA Method

A simple three-step process designed to help you step back and decide with clarity.

1. Define

Clarify the situation or choice you wish to examine. An honest question opens the way.

2. Reveal

Select your cards to discover the angles shaping your situation. Each card reveals a facet.

3. Clarify

The AI analyzes your cards and offers you a clear and strategic perspective.

Five Spread Types

From a flash of intuition to a complex decision, choose the depth that suits you.

Card of the Day

One card. One anchor. Start each day with a perspective. (1 card)

Yes / No

The direct answer when the question is clear-cut. Clear, sharp, immediate. (1 card)

Guidance

Three cards to understand a situation as a whole. Past, present, direction. (3 cards)

Path

Seven cards to choose between two options. Define Option A and Option B — ORA illuminates each path. (7 cards)

Deep

Ten cards for an in-depth reading. For questions that deserve to be truly explored. (10 cards)
