ORA

A decision-support companion for busy people. When you need a moment of clarity before a meeting, a conversation, or a choice — ORA is there.

Explore ORA

What ORA offers

ORA is a mobile and web application that helps users make clearer decisions through guided reflection and symbolic prompts. The application is currently completing final testing and is scheduled for release on the Apple App Store and Google Play. It is available in seven languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Japanese. ORA is a tool for reflection and entertainment, not a substitute for professional advice.

Quick Draw

A single-card pull with conversational interpretation — enough to shift perspective in under two minutes before a decision.

Conversational Clarity

Natural-language dialogue that helps you articulate what is really at stake, exploring angles you may not have considered.

Private Journal

Every session is saved in a personal journal, so you can track patterns, revisit insights, and observe how your thinking evolves.

How people use ORA

  • Morning Clarity — A two-minute draw to set intention before a packed day of meetings and decisions.
  • Decision Support — Exploring a work or personal dilemma through symbolic imagery and interpretive dialogue.
  • Creative Unblocking — Writers, founders, and artists use ORA to break through analysis paralysis and find fresh angles.
  • Evening Recap — Revisiting the day's draw to notice what played out, what shifted, and what to carry forward.

Business Model

ORA operates on a freemium model. Core features — including daily card draws and basic journal access — are available at no cost. Premium features are offered via monthly and annual subscription.

Philosophy

The value lies in the pause — the moment you stop reacting and start choosing. We build technology that creates that pause, then gets out of the way.

Technology

ORA is built on modern cloud infrastructure with a focus on security, data privacy, and responsive design. All user data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable U.S. regulations. The application is available on iOS, Android, and web.
