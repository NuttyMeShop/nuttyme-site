What ORA offers

ORA is a mobile and web application designed for individuals seeking a thoughtful, private space to explore questions, patterns, and possibilities through tarot and reflective dialogue. It is not a substitute for professional advice; it is a tool for personal exploration and entertainment.

Conversational Readings

Users draw cards and engage in natural-language dialogue to explore meanings, contexts, and personal relevance — guided by an AI that respects nuance and ambiguity.

Personal Journals

Every session is saved in a private journal, allowing users to track themes, revisit past readings, and observe their own evolving perspective over time.

Flexible Paths

From single-card daily draws to multi-card spreads, ORA supports a range of practices suited to beginners and experienced readers alike.

How people use ORA

  • Daily Reflection — A single card and a few minutes of quiet thought to start or end the day with intention.
  • Decision Support — Exploring different angles of a situation through symbolic imagery and interpretive dialogue.
  • Creative Prompt — Writers, artists, and thinkers use ORA to break through blocks and discover unexpected perspectives.
  • Self-Knowledge — Tracking recurring cards and themes to better understand personal patterns and growth areas.

Philosophy

ORA treats tarot as a mirror, not a crystal ball. The value lies in the reflection — the questions it surfaces, the stories it helps reframe, and the pause it creates in a noisy world. We build technology that respects this tradition while making it available to anyone with a phone.

Technology

ORA is built on modern cloud infrastructure with a focus on security, data privacy, and responsive design. All user data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable U.S. regulations. The application is available on iOS, Android, and web.
